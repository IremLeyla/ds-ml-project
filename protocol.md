| 2026-10-21 | Main evaluation metric | ROC-AUC is our main metric for comparing and tuning models (stratified cross-validation). PR-AUC is reported next to it as an imbalance check. | 2026-10-21 | ROC-AUC instead of F1 (plain explanation for the presentation) | The team confirmed ROC-AUC as the main metric after discussing an alternative that proposed F1 (with the F1-best threshold) as the headline number. The "Main evaluation metric" row from 2026-10-21 stays unchanged. For non-technical listeners we explain it like this: "STEG can only inspect a limited number of homes, so our model makes a priority list: who to visit first. ROC-AUC tells us how good that list is.
