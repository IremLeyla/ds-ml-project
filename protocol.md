| 2026-10-21 | Main evaluation metric | ROC-AUC is our main metric for comparing and tuning models (stratified cross-validation). PR-AUC is reported next to it as an imbalance check. F1 is not used to choose between models | STEG can only send inspectors to a limited number of homes, so the model's job is to put the clients most likely to commit fraud at the top of the list. The F1 in the assignment example assumes false alarms and misses cost the same; for STEG the limit is inspection capacity, so ranking quality matters more | Decided |
| 2026-10-21 | ROC-AUC instead of F1 (plain explanation for the presentation) | The team confirmed ROC-AUC as the main metric after discussing an alternative that proposed F1 (with the F1-best threshold) as the headline number.
